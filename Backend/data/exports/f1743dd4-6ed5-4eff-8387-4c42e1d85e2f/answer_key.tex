\textbf{Question 1 (1.00 pts).}~
What is a qubit in quantum computing?


\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\Alph*.}]

  \item \textbf{\textcolor{teal}{[CORRECT]}}  A physical two-level system described by |\ensuremath{\psi}\ensuremath{\rangle}= \ensuremath{\alpha} |0\ensuremath{\rangle}+ \ensuremath{\beta} |1\ensuremath{\rangle}

  \item A physical three-level system described by |\ensuremath{\psi}\ensuremath{\rangle}= \ensuremath{\alpha} |0\ensuremath{\rangle}+ \ensuremath{\beta} |1\ensuremath{\rangle}+ \ensuremath{\gamma} |2\ensuremath{\rangle}

  \item A physical one-level system described by |\ensuremath{\psi}\ensuremath{\rangle}= \ensuremath{\alpha} |0\ensuremath{\rangle}

  \item A physical four-level system described by |\ensuremath{\psi}\ensuremath{\rangle}= \ensuremath{\alpha} |0\ensuremath{\rangle}+ \ensuremath{\beta} |1\ensuremath{\rangle}+ \ensuremath{\gamma} |2\ensuremath{\rangle}+ \ensuremath{\delta} |3\ensuremath{\rangle}

\end{enumerate}



\textit{\small Explanation: A qubit is a fundamental unit of quantum information in quantum computing, described by a two-level system.}
